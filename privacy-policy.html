Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Growing MJ ("we", "us", "the site") is a static informational website. This policy explains what little data is involved in visiting it.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as our hosting provider, may automatically log standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) as part of normal web server operation. We do not have access to detailed visitor-level logs beyond aggregate, anonymized traffic statistics, if any. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details on how GitHub Pages handles this data.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set our own tracking cookies, and we do not currently run analytics software. If that changes in the future (for example, adding a privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics tool to understand which guides are useful), this policy will be updated to reflect it before any such tool goes live.

Affiliate links and third-party sites

Some links on this site are affiliate links, primarily through the Amazon Associates program (see our Affiliate Disclosure for details). When you click an affiliate link and land on a retailer's site, that retailer's own privacy policy and cookie practices apply, not ours. Amazon and other retailers may use cookies to track that a purchase originated from our link, which is how we earn a commission. We do not receive any personal information about you as part of this process — only aggregate reporting on clicks and sales attributed to our account.

Information you provide directly

At this time, the site does not have comment sections, account signups, or newsletter forms. If we add any of these in the future (for example, an email newsletter), this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected, how it's stored, and how you can opt out or request deletion.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally as the site evolves. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via the contact information on our About page.
