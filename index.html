The Four Loops, and the Plant They Are For

Nearly every problem a beginner posts a photo of is one of these four sitting out of range, and nearly every one of them is invisible without a number — a tent can look and smell perfect at 0.4 kPa while the plants quietly stop drinking. The fifth section is the plant itself, because nobody walks into a room thinking “my EC is wrong”; they walk in and see a yellow leaf.

Calculators

Every one runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, there is no sign-up, and each names the convention it uses — leaf temperature or air, the 500 scale or the 700 — as an input you control rather than an assumption buried in the code. That is usually why two grow calculators disagree.

  • VPD Calculator — temperature and humidity to vapour pressure deficit, with the leaf-temperature assumption as a field you control
  • Grow Tent Fan (CFM) Calculator — tent size to the fan you should actually buy, filter and ducting losses added back
  • DLI Calculator — PPFD and hours to daily light integral, with per-crop targets and a reverse mode
  • EC to PPM Converter — the 500, 640 and 700 scales at once, because they disagree by 40%
  • Deficiency Diagnosis — symptoms in, a ranked shortlist out, pH lockout checked first
