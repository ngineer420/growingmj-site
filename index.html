Grow something green, indoors, on purpose.

Growing MJ is a no-nonsense guide to indoor gardening — grow tents, LED lighting, hydroponics, herbs, and houseplants — written for people who want real results without buying a warehouse's worth of gear first.

Photo by Battledp, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0

Whether you're growing basil on a kitchen counter, setting up your first grow tent, or building a hydroponic system in a spare closet, the goal here is the same: fewer dead plants, less wasted money, and a setup that actually fits your space. Every guide below is written from a beginner's perspective, with specific gear categories called out so you know exactly what to shop for.
