Guides

An indoor grow is four control loops wearing a trench coat. Everything here is filed under the loop it belongs to.

Light in (DLI), water through (VPD), air through (CFM), and salt in solution (EC). Nearly every problem a beginner posts a photo of is one of those four sitting out of range, and nearly every one of them is invisible without a number — which is why each section here is paired with the calculator that produces its number. The fifth section is the plant itself: the work you do with your hands, and what to read when the plant is the one telling you something.
